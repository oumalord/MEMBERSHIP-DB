# Tests

## Test 1 - Individual signs up and profile persists after reload [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: individual sign-up, authentication, database create, persistence after reload
Description: Verifies the individual sign-up flow creates a durable member profile.

Steps:
1. Sign in
2. On the landing screen, under Sign Up, choose Individual
3. Enter the full name "Jane Individual" and create the account
4. Refresh the page

Expected: The Member Portal sidebar still shows "Jane Individual" and the "My Membership" page after the refresh.

## Test 2 - Institution signs up and adds a student to its roster
Viewport: desktop
Covers: institution sign-up, student creation, database persistence, roster display
Description: Verifies an institution can register and manage its own student roster.

Steps:
1. Sign in
2. On the landing screen, under Sign Up, choose Institution
3. Enter the institution name "Nairobi Girls Institution" and create the account
4. Click "+ Add Student" and add a student named "Student One"
5. Refresh the page

Expected: "Student One" appears in the Students roster list after the refresh.

## Test 3 - First admin sign-in becomes Super Admin and can add a member with an invoice
Viewport: desktop
Covers: admin bootstrap as Super Admin, member creation, invoice issuance, edit-rights gating
Description: Verifies the first person to use Admin sign-in becomes Super Admin with full editing rights and can manage members and billing.

Steps:
1. Sign in
2. On the landing screen, under Sign In, choose Admin
3. Navigate to Members and add a member named "Admin Added Member"
4. Open "Admin Added Member" and issue an invoice for amount 300

Expected: The sidebar shows the "Super Admin" badge, "Admin Added Member" appears in the Members list, and the issued KES 300 invoice appears on the member's statement.

## Test 4 - Institution adds a custom category on mobile
Viewport: mobile
Covers: institution custom category creation, category persistence
Description: Verifies an institution can define its own category alongside the default branches.

Steps:
1. Sign in
2. On the landing screen, under Sign Up, choose Institution and create an account named "Mobile Test Institution"
3. Switch to the Categories tab
4. Add a custom category named "Special Troop"

Expected: "Special Troop" appears under Your Custom Categories.

## Test 5 - Reject an empty name on individual sign-up
Viewport: mobile
Covers: required-name validation, invalid-write guardrail on the individual sign-up form
Description: Verifies invalid input is blocked without creating an account.

Steps:
1. Sign in
2. On the landing screen, under Sign Up, choose Individual
3. Leave the name field empty and click "Create Account"

Expected: A validation message is shown and the sign-up form remains open without creating an account.

## Test 6 - Members and institution staff register for events and see notices
Viewport: desktop
Covers: event listing, individual self-registration, institution-scoped student registration, superadmin notices
Description: Verifies event registration is available to members and institution staff, and superadmin notices are visible across portals.

Steps:
1. Sign in as Super Admin, schedule an event, then publish a notice from Messages
2. Sign in as an individual member and register for the event
3. Refresh the member portal and check the event shows as registered and the notice is visible
4. Sign in as an institution account with at least one student, open Events, select that student, and register them
5. Sign in as another admin account and open Messages

Expected: The member can self-register once, institution staff can register students from their own roster, and the notice is visible to the member, institution account, and admin account. Only the Super Admin can publish notices.
